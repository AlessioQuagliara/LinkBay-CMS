FAQs

The questions every SEO asks in the first two minutes of a demo.

How is this different from Google Search Console?

Search Console gives you the data — thousands of rows and no particular order. LinkBayCMS gives you the five things to fix Monday morning, ranked by how much they're worth and how likely they are to recover. It's not another archive; it's a shortlist.

How do you know a page is really declining, and not just noisy?

A page only makes the list if it clears a minimum relevance threshold, has dropped at least 30% and at least 20 clicks/month, held that drop for two consecutive months, and is falling faster than the rest of your site in the same period. That last check alone removes most of the false alarms — it's the difference between "your page is broken" and "Google shipped an update and your whole site moved."

Do I need to install anything on my site?

No. LinkBayCMS is not a CMS and doesn't touch your site — it reads your existing Google Search Console data with read-only access. Nothing to embed, no plugin, no code changes.

Is this built for e-commerce or SaaS marketing sites too?

Not really — it's built specifically for publishers and editorial sites with 500 to 20,000 indexed pages and at least 20,000 organic clicks a month, where organic search is the main source of revenue. Below that volume, declines are hard to tell apart from noise, and our output gets less reliable. We'd rather tell you that upfront than sign you up and have you churn in month two.

Is my Search Console data safe?

We connect via Google's standard read-only access — we can see your performance data, we can't change anything on your property. We never invent numbers: click figures come straight from Search Console, and any revenue estimate is only shown if you provide it, always labeled as an estimate.
