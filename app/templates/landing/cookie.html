This Cookie Policy explains how LinkBayCMS uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them. For how we handle personal data more broadly, see our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They let the site remember your actions and preferences (such as your consent choices) and help us understand how the Service is used. We also use equivalent technologies such as local storage.

2. Categories we use

Strictly necessary

Required for the site to function — for example, to keep you signed in, to protect against cross-site request forgery, and to remember your cookie choices. These are always on and cannot be switched off.

Analytics (optional)

Help us understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the Service so we can improve it. These run only if you allow them.

Marketing (optional)

Used to measure campaigns and show relevant content off-site. These run only if you allow them.

3. Cookies and storage we set

Name Type Purpose Duration
session Necessary Keeps you signed in and secures your session. Session
csrf / session token Necessary Protects forms against cross-site request forgery. Session
lb_consent Necessary Stores your cookie preferences so we don't ask again. 180 days

Analytics and marketing cookies are listed here only if and when they are enabled. No optional cookie is set before you give consent.

4. Managing your choices

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept all, reject non-essential cookies, or choose per category. You can change your choice at any time using the Manage cookie preferences button above. You can also delete cookies through your browser settings; note that blocking necessary cookies may break parts of the Service.

5. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as the Service evolves. We will update the "Last updated" date above, and material changes to the categories will prompt the consent banner again.
