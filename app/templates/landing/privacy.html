This Privacy Policy explains how {{ operator }} ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use LinkBayCMS (the "Service"). We act as the data controller for the personal data described here.

1. Data we collect

Account data

  • Email address and authentication data (a hashed password) when you register;
  • Basic account settings and preferences.

Google Search Console data

With your explicit authorization (Google OAuth, read-only scope), we access and store performance metrics for the properties you connect — such as URLs, clicks, impressions, positions, and dates. We keep a history of these metrics so you retain data beyond Google's 16-month window. We never receive the ability to modify your Google property.

Usage and technical data

  • Log data such as IP address, browser type, and pages accessed, used for security and to operate the Service;
  • Cookies and similar technologies, as described in our Cookie Policy.

2. How we use your data

  • To provide the core Service: analyzing Search Console data and producing prioritized insights and suggestions;
  • To create and secure your account and authenticate you;
  • To communicate with you about the Service (e.g. transactional emails);
  • To maintain, debug, and improve the Service;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Contract — to provide the Service you signed up for;
  • Consent — for non-essential cookies and, where applicable, connecting your Google data; you may withdraw consent at any time;
  • Legitimate interests — to secure and improve the Service, balanced against your rights;
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose data by law.

4. AI processing and subprocessors

To generate suggestions, a compact summary of the relevant page metrics may be sent to our AI processing provider (DeepSeek) solely to return the analysis. We share only the data needed for that feature and do not sell your data. We use trusted service providers (for example, hosting and infrastructure) that process data on our behalf under appropriate agreements. Where processing occurs outside the EEA, we use appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Google user data

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google Search Console data only to provide and improve the user-facing features of the Service, and we do not use it for advertising or sell it to third parties.

6. Data retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. We retain Search Console history to provide the long-history feature; you can request deletion at any time. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Request portability of data you provided;
  • Withdraw consent at any time;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at {{ contact_email }}. You can also revoke LinkBayCMS's access to your Google data from your Google Account security settings.

8. Security

We use technical and organizational measures — including encryption in transit, hashed passwords, and restricted access — to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to respond promptly to any incident.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, take reasonable steps to notify you.

11. Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact us at {{ contact_email }}.
